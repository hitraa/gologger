# gologger Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive project community. Participation
in discussions, issues, pull requests, and other project spaces is expected to
be professional and considerate.

## Expected behavior

- Be welcoming and respectful of differing experience, identity, and viewpoints.
- Give and receive technical feedback constructively; critique ideas, not people.
- Respect privacy and avoid sharing personal information without consent.
- Keep discussion focused on the project and make room for others to participate.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, deliberate intimidation,
unwanted sexual attention, and publishing another person's private information
are not acceptable. Repeated disruptive conduct or knowingly abusive project
communications are also out of bounds.

## Enforcement and reporting

Project maintainers may remove content, restrict participation, or otherwise
take action proportionate to the conduct, including a temporary or permanent
ban from project spaces. To report an incident or request a review, email
Harshal Khairnar at harshal@hitraa.com. Reports will be handled discreetly and
shared only as needed to investigate and respond.
